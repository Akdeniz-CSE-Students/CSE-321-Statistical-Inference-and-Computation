# Topic 3 — Probability

**Previous:** [Topic 2](02-describing-sets-of-data.md) · **Next:** [Topic 4](04-discrete-random-variables.md)

## Goals
- Compute probabilities of unions, intersections and complements.
- Apply conditional probability, independence and Bayes' rule.

## Key concepts
- Sample space, event, the three probability axioms.
- **Additive rule** `P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B)`; mutually exclusive events.
- **Conditional probability** `P(A|B) = P(A∩B)/P(B)`.
- **Multiplicative rule** `P(A∩B) = P(A|B)P(B)`; **independence** `P(A|B) = P(A)`.
- Mutually exclusive ≠ independent — the classic exam trap.
- **Bayes' rule** `P(Bᵢ|A) = P(A|Bᵢ)P(Bᵢ) / Σ P(A|Bⱼ)P(Bⱼ)`.
- Counting: permutations, combinations, the multiplicative rule.
